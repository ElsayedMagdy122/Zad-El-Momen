# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive space for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expectations

* Treat everyone with respect and patience.
* Keep discussions constructive and focused on the project.
* Give and accept feedback professionally.
* Focus on what is best for the app and the users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or offensive remarks.
* Publishing private information of others without consent.
* Any behavior that disrupts the collaborative environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for keeping conversations respectful and will take appropriate action if issues arise, including editing or removing comments, closing discussions, or blocking abusive accounts.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the repository maintainers through GitHub issues or by email.
